Workout Plan

Gymnastics-based strength, circuits and boxing conditioning — the same training philosophy behind the Spider-Man prep, scaled to your window.

Your 2-hour block, budgeted

You don't need to fill the full 1.5–2 hours with hard training. Past ~90–100 minutes of intense work, returns drop and recovery suffers — that's true even for actors on a movie prep. Use the rest of the window for mobility, not more sets.

10 min warm-up & joint mobility 55–65 min main session (lifts / circuit / boxing) 10 min core finisher 15 min cooldown & stretch = ~95 min hard budget, rest of window optional light mobility
